# 🌾 KrishiSetu AI

### Kisan se Bazaar tak – Sab kuch ek Setu par

KrishiSetu AI is a frontend-based agriculture ecosystem platform designed to connect farmers, consumers, financial institutions, and government services on a single digital platform.

Built as a hackathon prototype, it provides farmers with direct market access, loan assistance, government scheme information, weather updates, GI product tracking, and AI-powered support.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 👨‍🌾 Farmer Dashboard
- Farmer Profile Management
- Crop Information
- Income Tracking
- Weather Updates
- Market Price Monitoring

### 🛒 Marketplace
- 60+ Agricultural Products
- Buy/Sell Functionality
- Product Search & Filtering
- Farmer-wise Product Listing

### 💰 Smart Loan System
- Loan Application Form
- Eligibility Check
- EMI Calculator
- Approval Simulation

### 🏛 Government Schemes
- 10+ Agricultural Schemes
- Eligibility Verification
- Document Requirements
- Apply Feature

### 🔗 GI Product Tracker
- Product Origin Verification
- Farmer Information
- Product Journey Tracking
- Authenticity Check

### 🤖 AI Chatbot
- Agriculture Related FAQs
- Instant Guidance
- Editable Knowledge Base

### 👨‍💼 Super Admin Dashboard
- Manage Marketplace
- Manage Loans
- Manage Schemes
- Manage Weather Data
- Manage GI Products
- Manage Chatbot Responses
- Analytics Dashboard

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Farmers face challenges such as:
- Low income due to middlemen
- Lack of market transparency
- Limited access to loans and schemes
- Difficulty verifying agricultural products

Consumers face:
- Fake agricultural products
- Lack of traceability
- Trust issues in food sourcing

---

## 💡 Solution

KrishiSetu AI provides a unified platform where farmers can:
- Sell directly to buyers
- Access financial support
- Discover government schemes
- Verify product authenticity
- Receive AI-assisted guidance
